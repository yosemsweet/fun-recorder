# 01.001 — Tap Waveform to Seek

**Milestone:** 1 (Audio Capture & Region Selection)

## Summary

Tapping anywhere on the waveform in the editing view moves the playhead to that position.

## Motivation

The waveform is the primary spatial representation of the recording. Users naturally expect to tap a point in the waveform to jump to it — especially when reviewing a recording to find where to place region handles. Without this, the only way to move the playhead is to start playback from wherever it currently sits.

## Behaviour

- A tap anywhere on the `WaveformRegionView` (outside the region handles) seeks to the corresponding time position
- The playhead moves immediately regardless of whether playback is active
- If playback is active, it continues from the new position
- Tap position maps linearly from the view's x-coordinate to the recording duration, accounting for zoom and scroll offset

## Out of Scope

- Scrubbing (continuous drag to seek) — separate feature if needed
- Seeking outside the current region bounds — clamp to region start/end or allow full-file seek (decide at implementation time)
